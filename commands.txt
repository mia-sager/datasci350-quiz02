title: "Movies"

This dataset explores some of the biggest box-office hit movies in history. It includes information on the year it was released, franchise, the film's budget, the film's revenue, the length of the film, as well as its public rating on IMDB. There is further analysis on the relationship between a film's [budget and revenue](budget-revenue.qmd), a film's [runtime and rating](runtime-rating.qmd), and the revenue of the [Bond films](bond.qmd).

This page will explore the relationship between budgets and revenues for well-known box office films. Based on the scatterplot @fig-budget-revenue, we can see that there is a positive correlation between large budgets and large revenue. 

```{python}
#| echo: true
#| label: fig-budget-revenue
#| fig-cap: "Budgets vs. Revenues"
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

films = pd.read_csv("films.csv")

plt.scatter(films["budget_musd"], films["revenue_musd"])
plt.xlabel("Budget (millions of USD)")
plt.ylabel("Revenue (millions of USD)")
plt.title("Budget vs Revenue")
plt.show()
```

```{python}

#| fig-cap: "Runtime vs. Ratings"
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

films = pd.read_csv("films.csv")

plt.scatter(films["runtime_min"], films["imdb_rating"])
plt.xlabel("Runtime (minutes)")
plt.ylabel("IMDB Rating (0 to 10)")
plt.title("Runtime vs Rating")
plt.show()
```
